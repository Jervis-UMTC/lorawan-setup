Problem Statement 02 / 10

The Agricultural Field Data Gap

Why traditional farm monitoring methods fail to deliver real-time field visibility.

The Limits of Standard Weather Stations

• Regional weather stations only measure ambient air on elevated towers miles away.

• They completely miss localized canopy microclimates, greenhouse humidity pockets, and low-lying frost valleys where crops actually live and grow.

High Costs & Commercial Vendor Lock-In Cellular SIM

• Proprietary commercial farm sensors require expensive per-device SIM subscriptions ($5–$10/month per node).

• Commercial vendors often lock farm data behind proprietary cloud paywalls, making wide-scale deployment cost-prohibitive for large properties.

Wireless Architecture 03 / 10

Long-Range, Zero-Subscription Field Coverage

Establishing a private, low-cost LoRaWAN wireless network across farm terrain.

Multi-Kilometer Canopy Penetration

• Long-range LoRaWAN radio signals travel 5 to 15 kilometers across farm terrain.

• Low-frequency radio signals easily pass through dense crop foliage, tree lines, and greenhouse structures where higher-frequency Wi-Fi drops within 50 meters.

Zero Monthly Data Fees & Complete Privacy

• One central base station gateway manages hundreds of field sensors for $0 monthly data fees.

• Keeps operating expenses minimal while securing 100% data ownership without relying on third-party cloud vendors.

Core Value Proposition

Soil NPK Sensing 08 / 10

Real-Time Soil NPK Nutrient Intelligence

Replacing slow off-site lab testing with live root-zone soil monitoring using RAKwireless sensors.

The Traditional Soil Testing Problem

• Standard soil testing requires taking manual soil core samples, mailing them to off-site labs, and waiting 2–3 weeks for lab results.

• By the time results arrive, soil conditions have shifted, leading to blind over-fertilization, high fertilizer expenses, and chemical runoff.

RAKwireless Soil NPK Sensing & ROI RAKwireless NPK Sensor

• Live Nutrient Tracking: Integrating the industrial RAKwireless RS485 Soil NPK Sensor allows measuring Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), and Potassium (K) directly in root soil in real time.

• Direct Cost Savings: Allows applying fertilizer only when and where crops actually need it—reducing fertilizer expenses, boosting crop yield, and protecting long-term soil health.
